Meet Miss Paty

Experienced care that still feels personal.

At Guardería Mi Bendición, children are known, supported, and encouraged at their own pace. Our small-group setting gives each child room to build confidence while forming close, steady relationships.

Every day combines play, early learning, movement, creativity, and rest in a comfortable home environment. Families receive care grounded in patience, clear routines, and more than three decades of experience in education.
